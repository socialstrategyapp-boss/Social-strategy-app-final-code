Currently stops fire at a set price — but in a fast market you fill 0.3–1.5% below your stop. PATTERN MEMORY ENGINE
🔄 SELF-LEARNING SIGNAL WEIGHT
After every 50 trades, the bot re-weights its signals based on which combinations actually made money in the most recent market conditions. A signal that was strong 3 months ago may be weak now. This engine detects that drift and rebalances automatically — like a trader learning from experience without emotion.
Estimated impact: +6 to +12% accuracy improvement over 90 days
CROSS-BOT CORRELATION GUARD
🛡️ PORTFOLIO-LEVEL RISK
